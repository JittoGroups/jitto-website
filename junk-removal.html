We handle nearly all non-hazardous residential, commercial and renovation waste across Simcoe County.

Household & attic clutterBoxes, books, clothes, exercise equipment, tools, holiday decorations

Restricted materials

By Ontario environmental regulations, we are unable to transport toxic, biological or pressurized hazardous materials:

  • Wet paints, stains or liquid solvents
  • Hazardous chemicals, motor oil or car batteries
  • Biological or medical waste
  • Asbestos-containing materials
  • Pressurized propane tanks or explosives

Need guidance on household hazardous waste? Call our team and we'll gladly point you to the right Simcoe County depot.

Green disposal & stewardship

Simcoe County eco-diversion & charity donations

Not everything belongs in a landfill. Before taking items to disposal transfer facilities, our team sorts salvageable wooden furniture, functional appliances, books and household goods.

We work with local Simcoe County charities, community reuse centres and metal recyclers to keep as much as possible out of municipal landfills. You get a clutter-free home while giving usable items a second life.
